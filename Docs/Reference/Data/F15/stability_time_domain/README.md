# F-15 research source stability in the time domain — dataset (WP-3E)

> **Research source model only** (`F15_AFIT_BAUMANN_DAVISON_MACH06_20K_RESEARCH`, the AFIT/Baumann/Davison FUNX, no CAS, fixed stabilator). These are fixed-step RK4 integrations of that model's own equations, off the body. They are **not** the real F-15, NASA 836, the production FCS or a Unity Rigidbody, and nothing here was flown. Method and findings: `Docs/Reference/F15_RESEARCH_TIME_DOMAIN_STABILITY_V1.0.md` (Goal A) and `Docs/Reference/F15_RESEARCH_STABILITY_CONFLICT_AUDIT_V1.0.md` (Goal B).

**Origin.**
- **Generated by:** `MavF15ResearchTimeDomainValidation.ExportDataset`, in Unity 6000.3.16f1, through the FDM batch adapter (`-fdmMethod ExportDataset`).
- **Extracted with:** `../stability/extract_from_log.py <log> <this folder>`.
- **Deterministic:** two separate Unity runs produced byte-identical files.
- **Scope:** the WP-3D dataset (`../stability/`) and the Table VII dataset (`../table_vii/`) are not modified. `[T13]` checks them byte for byte.

**Units.**
- States in the physical units: α, β, θ, φ in degrees in the symmetry-breaking file; rad, rad/s and ft/s in the perturbation columns (`d_*`).
- Rates σ in 1/s, frequencies ω in rad/s, t in s.
- **Amplitude** a: the largest perturbed component in scaled units (1 rad, 1 rad, 1 rad/s ×3, 1 rad, 1 rad, V₀).

**Case groups.**
- **A:** stable symmetric point 20 (α 15.4°), all five modes.
- **B:** the printed-CMMQ band, points 47, 5 and 8 (unstable), and point 9 past the located Hopf.
- **C:** stable turning point 150, all five modes, and point 117's 8 rad/s mode.
- **D:** both sides of each fold (123/125, 135/137, 181/183, 193/195) and a saddle interior point (130).
- **E:** the printed limit points 124, 136, 182. Point 194 is print-damaged.
- **F:** the symmetric branch across the pitchfork (V 372.4, 377.3, 377.6, 382.4), from WP-3B solves.
- **G:** the turning branches next to the pitchfork (φ ±20°, ±2°), from WP-3C solves.

**Measurement method.** The perturbation is the antisymmetric pair x* ± a·d, with d the WP-3D eigenvector (Re(v) for a complex pair). Rates are the slope of ln|η| inside the local linear window. The eigenvalue is never an input to the integration. The agreement bands are NUMERICAL: 1 %·|λ| + 3u + 1e-7 for rates, 0.5 %·|Im λ| + 3u for frequencies.

**Limit points (group E).** `rate_agrees` compares with the WP-3D eigenvalue, which is itself inside the zero band there. The test for these rows is near-neutrality (`[T7]`), not the rate band. Point 124 reads `false` against its predicted −6.8e-6 while being inside the zero band 1.4e-5.
